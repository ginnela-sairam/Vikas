# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and
respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, and personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[sairamginnela@gmail.com](mailto:sairamginnela@gmail.com). Reports will be
reviewed and handled as fairly as possible.
